CSRS Selenium Bot

Log in to load current PE and NSTP registration subjects. Review your choices before manual enlistment, or enable auto-enlist to skip review.

When enabled before login, checked saved subjects are submitted as soon as Registration loads. During a session, turning this on submits all checked subjects; checking another subject while it is on submits that subject immediately. This setting is saved in this browser and is off by default.

Log in to open Registration

Credentials are sent to this computer's local app, then submitted by Selenium to the student portal. They are not saved to a file or browser storage. The login fields stay populated while this app page is open, including after the Selenium session closes; reloading or closing the app page clears them. All requested Selenium windows are tiled on the left and reach the login form before credentials are entered. They then run in parallel using the same credentials; the first to load Registration stays open and the others close. If all attempts fail, their windows remain open. If a new login form appears, Selenium waits until its fields are ready before re-entering them.
